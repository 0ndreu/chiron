## Milestones

A month is 4 course weeks from week 1. Checked in the Sunday check of weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 28.
Missing one is a reason to talk, not to rush.
Chiron reports the facts; the user decides.

- Week 4 (month 1) — the trial verdict:
  - module 1 closed;
  - the pipeline ran end to end without manual fixes (data → paper → one full lab cycle);
  - the money part, on the first billing cycle (it starts at the Portal purchase, before week 1): the credits lasted to
    the first renewal, and none of the user's jobs was skipped for lack of money. The user checks it: the last Sunday
    usage report before the renewal (its "projection to" date) and `/topup` the day before. At the W4 check, ask for
    the result and write it here; if the renewal comes after W4, the user adds it then;
  - the user completed at least 2 of 4 weeks (fewer than half → talk honestly about stopping).
- Week 8 (month 2): module 2 closed.
- Week 12 (month 3): `engine.py` and `checks.py` audited; lookahead lesson done.
- Week 16 (month 4): module 3 gate passed; the user has audited the harness (the result code line by line, the rest by
  behaviour, the planted bug found).
- Week 20 (month 5): first strategy evaluated through the lab; decision on the user's own IBKR paper account made.
- Week 28 (month 7): trading plan v2 + retro.
